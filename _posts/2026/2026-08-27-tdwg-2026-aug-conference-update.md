## Calling out for Contributed Oral Sessions moderators
The TDWG 2026 Organizing Committee is **seeking in-person volunteers to moderate** the Contributed Oral sessions, and two additional sessions, for the conference.

We are looking to fill the roles of moderator and co-moderator for each Contributed Oral session and are happy for you to volunteer for more than one session. As a moderator, you will contribute to the successful running of that session and to the conference as a whole.

Your name will be listed in the conference program, you will gain more exposure in an international conference, and you will improve your networking opportunities.

The moderator responsibilities include: introducing the session, making announcements, introducing each speaker, managing Q\&A and bringing the session to a close.

The co-moderator responsibilities include: timekeeping to make sure presentations run on time, monitoring online chat and room for questions, and helping the moderator as directed.

A summary of the sessions that need moderators can be found below:
