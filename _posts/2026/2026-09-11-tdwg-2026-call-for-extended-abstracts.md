We are pleased to announce the call for extended abstracts (mini-papers) for TDWG2026 to be published in [*Biodiversity Information Science and Standards (BISS)*](https://biss.pensoft.net/).

See instructions below:

**Submitting authors:** [https://www.tdwg.org/journal/abstract-submission-instructions/](https://www.tdwg.org/journal/abstract-submission-instructions/)

**Session Organisers/Reviewers:** [https://www.tdwg.org/journal/reviewer-instructions/](https://www.tdwg.org/journal/reviewer-instructions/)

## About BISS

BISS is an open-access journal published by [Pensoft Publishers](https://pensoft.net/), who are a valued sponsor of TDWG 2026\. Please say hello to them at their stand in Oslo to find out more about their open-access peer-reviewed [journal portfolio](https://pensoft.net/browse-journals).

## Dates

**Deadline** for extended abstract submission is **9 October 2026**

## Why to submit an extended abstract

Wondering why you might want to submit an extended abstract when your original abstract was already accepted for presentation at the conference? See all the reasons why here: [https://www.tdwg.org/journal/why-an-extended-abstract/](https://www.tdwg.org/journal/why-an-extended-abstract/)

## Eligibility

To eligible for submission the abstract must:

* Be accepted for presentation at TDWG2026,
* Be scheduled for presentation / have been presented at TDWG2026 either as a talk or a poster
* Have the presenter registered for the conference.

Note that the person submitting the extended abstract to BISS does not have to be either the presenter or the original first author who submitted the abstract to TDWG 2026\. It is important that whoever is the new first / submitting author be available to handle reviews in a timely manner.

## Cost

Extended abstracts for TDWG 2026 will be published **free of charge** as a benefit of registration.
