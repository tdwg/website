# Darwin Core public review Cycle 3
## Public review

This is a reminder about the most recent Darwin Core public review. Proposals for this third public review cycle of 2026 were accepted up to 1 November. Commentary on any proposal is always welcome, but only the proposals in milestone [23](https://github.com/tdwg/dwc/milestone/23) are being considered for ratification. Proposals that do not have consensus by **15 December** will be deferred to the subsequent public review. Proposals that have no dissenting opinions **between 16 December and 15 January** will be forwarded to the Executive Committee with a recommendation for ratification by **1 February 2027**.

Expressions of support for proposals are as welcome as constructive criticism. The goal, as always, is to improve Darwin Core for all. Your participation helps to make that happen.
